# Approved goddess master references

The seven PNG files under `assets/goddesses/masters/` are immutable user-approved sources. The manifest records their SHA-256 hashes. WebP copies are separate display derivatives. CSS crops display the same approved artwork; no character redesign or synthesized portraits have been introduced.

The current written canon governs kingdom names: Lena — Armonia; Nilera — Serapha. The supplied sheets retain their original lettering. Nora is oldest; Yuna is second-born and leads.

For any future generated artwork, compare face, eyes, hair, complexion, proportions, costume and symbols directly with the master before acceptance. Nora has completely black eyes. Kira has black eyes and human ears. Dara retains her muscular physique. Nilera retains her tan complexion, curly hair and fuller physique. Lena retains copper hair, green eyes and pointed ears. Never overwrite a master or homogenize the sisters.
