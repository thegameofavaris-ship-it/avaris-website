AVARISTHE CREATION MYTH

Avaris was not created merely to be a world. It was created in harmony, so that things unlike one another might breathe together as a whole.

Beneath the same sky stretch seven kingdoms, divided by seas, mountains, forests, and lands where life learned to exist in different ways. Each has its own earth, its own nature. And yet, for centuries, the seven kingdoms learned to breathe within the same harmony; remaining different from one another while continuing to exist as inseparable parts of the absolute order of the universe.

Cities were built where Mother Nature allowed them to stand, and roads wound around the mountains.

Before seeking dominion over the lands they inhabited, people learned to understand them.

Some raised their eyes to the heavens and learned to read the stars.Some stood before the fire and gave shape to iron.Some searched for truth within the eyes of others.Some learned to heal wounds.Some listened to the song of nature.Some saw the shadows that souls left behind in the world.And some learned to forge a new order from the destruction of fire.

Yet none sought to become existence itself.

Together, they were whole.

Long before borders, before thrones, and before wars for crowns were written into history in blood, there were seven sisters.

Their names had not yet become prayers.No temples had been raised in their honor, nor had their likenesses been carved into stone.Kings and queens did not yet rule over the lands that had been their gift.

It was they who blessed the first harmony of Avaris.

Each left a fragment of her own nature within the world.

And so seven distinct powers entered the veins of Avaris. But none of these powers was created to stand above the others, nor was any of them meant to gather within a single hand.

Time passed.

Gifts became inheritance.Inheritance became duty.And duty became power.

And like all things placed into human hands, power gathered new meanings with time.

Generations followed one another. The names of the sisters endured in prayer, while the relics they had left behind passed from one bloodline to the next. Yet truths once known in the same way by all began to change, little by little, with every voice that told them.

A word was forgotten.A name disappeared into the dust of history.A victory grew greater in the telling.A defeat was buried in silence.

And with time, the line between history and memory began to fade.

Even today, the roads of the seven kingdoms still meet at the heart of Avaris. Beneath the same roofs, people trade, learn from one another's knowledge, carry old tales and poems beyond new borders, and remember wars they themselves never witnessed.

The temples tell one version of the past.The libraries tell another.And some families still whisper stories to their children that can be found in neither.

Yet a few truths have survived every war, every ruler, and every telling:

Seven kingdoms.Seven bloodlines entrusted with seven relics.Seven names older than any crown.

And above them all, the same ancient law—broken and restored since the first day the world was created:

One harmony of the universe.
